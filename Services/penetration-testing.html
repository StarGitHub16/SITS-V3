The Summit IT Penetration Test

Our advantage lies in a methodical, intelligence-driven approach that mirrors modern threat actors.

Reconnaissance & Planning

expand_more

We work with you to define the scope and rules of engagement. Our team then gathers intelligence on your systems to plan a tailored attack strategy, ensuring a safe and effective test.

Controlled Exploitation

expand_more

Once vulnerabilities are identified, our ethical hackers attempt to exploit them under controlled conditions to determine the real-world risk level and potential impact on your business.

Strategic Reporting & Debrief

expand_more

You receive a comprehensive report detailing our findings, the risk level of each vulnerability, and clear, prioritized steps for remediation. We debrief with your team to ensure you understand the results and the path forward.

Our Offensive Security Offerings

We offer a full suite of penetration testing services to identify vulnerabilities across your entire technology and human landscape, giving you a 360-degree view of your security posture.

External & Internal Network Testing

expand_more

We simulate attacks from both outside your firewall (external) and from a compromised position inside your network (internal). This uncovers vulnerabilities like weak passwords, unpatched systems, and misconfigurations that could lead to a breach.

Web Application Penetration Testing

expand_more

Your web apps are a gateway to your data. We test for common vulnerabilities like SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and authentication flaws to ensure your customer and business data remains secure. Is your login page truly secure?

Social Engineering Experiments

expand_more

Humans are often the weakest link. We conduct controlled phishing, vishing (voice phishing), and physical access tests to gauge your team's security awareness and identify areas for crucial training and policy improvements.
