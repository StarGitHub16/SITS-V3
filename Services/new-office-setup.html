Methodology

From Blueprint to
Day One.

We manage the complexity of physical IT deployment so you can focus on running your business. Our process is transparent, documented, and rigorous.

01

Blueprint Analysis & Survey

Before we pull a single cable, we analyze your architectural plans. We perform a physical site walkthrough to identify cable paths, MDF/IDF locations, HVAC considerations, and power requirements.

02

Infrastructure Design

Our engineers design a comprehensive network map. We produce rack elevations, WAP placement heatmaps, and a precise hardware procurement list tailored to your budget and future growth.

03

Installation & Deployment

Execution time. Our certified team pulls cable, mounts hardware, terminates connections, and organizes racks with military-grade precision. We coordinate with your general contractor to stay on schedule.

04

Testing & Handover

We don't just leave. We certify every cable run, stress-test the Wi-Fi, and label every port. You receive a full documentation binder and network diagram before we sign off.
