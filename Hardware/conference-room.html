TECHNICAL ARCHITECT OVERVIEW

For huddle rooms: Single high-definition interactive display (e.g., 55-inch). For conference rooms: Dual 65-inch 4K displays or a single large format interactive display. Boardrooms: Video wall solutions, professional-grade laser projectors, or multiple 85-inch 4K screens for commanding visual presence.

Acoustically optimized soundbars for smaller spaces. For larger rooms: DSP-driven (Digital Signal Processing) ceiling-mounted speakers with advanced echo cancellation and noise reduction. Boardrooms feature immersive soundscapes with discreet, high-fidelity audio systems, ensuring every nuance of speech is captured and reproduced.

Integrated webcam solutions for huddle rooms. Conference rooms benefit from 4K PTZ (Pan-Tilt-Zoom) cameras with auto-framing and speaker-tracking capabilities. Executive boardrooms demand dual-camera setups with AI-powered participant framing and ultra-wide angle lenses for comprehensive coverage.

Simplified, one-touch control panels for seamless room activation and content sharing. Full integration with environmental controls (lighting, shades) and wireless presentation systems for a frictionless user experience across all room sizes.
