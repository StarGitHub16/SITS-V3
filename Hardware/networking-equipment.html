Structured Cabling & Infrastructure expand_more

Technical Specs:

  • Fiber optic cables (OS1, OS2, OM1, OM2, OM3, OM4, OM5) for high bandwidth and long distances.
  • Copper cables (Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a, Cat7) for reliable Ethernet connections.
  • Pre-terminated fiber and copper solutions for faster deployment.
  • Rack systems and enclosures for organized and secure equipment housing.
  • Cable management solutions to prevent tangles and improve airflow.

Use Cases:

  • Office Building Infrastructure: Robust cabling for workstations, Wi-Fi access points, and VoIP systems.
  • Industrial Environments: Durable, shielded cabling for harsh conditions and specific machinery.
  • Campus Networks: Integrated solutions connecting multiple buildings with fiber backbones.
