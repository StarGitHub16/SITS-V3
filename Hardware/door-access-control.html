Common Questions

How long does installation take? expand_more
Typical installation for a standard office (2-5 doors) takes 1-2 days. We minimize disruption by running cabling during off-hours if required. Our team handles everything from mounting hardware to configuring your cloud dashboard.
Does it work if the internet goes down? expand_more
Yes. Our controllers have local storage that caches credentials. Even if your internet connection is lost, authorized users can still gain entry, and logs will sync once connectivity is restored.
Can I use my existing key cards? expand_more
In many cases, yes. Our systems support standard frequencies (125kHz and 13.56MHz). We can test your existing credentials during our site survey to confirm compatibility, potentially saving you the cost of re-issuing cards.
